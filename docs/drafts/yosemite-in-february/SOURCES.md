# Sources: yosemite-in-february (DRAFT, unpublished)

Every page below was fetched and read on **October 16, 2026** unless marked otherwise. "Canon" means the claim also appears, from the cited source, in an article already on the site.

## Why this topic, and why it does not cannibalize

- The month series has `yosemite-in-january` and `yosemite-in-march` but no February. `seo-data.json` already answers February questions only inside other pieces (the Mariposa Grove FAQ calls a grove walk "one of the quietest good days available in Yosemite in February").
- `horsetail-fall-firefall` owns the firefall itself (the physics, the three conditions, odds, history, photography). This draft owns the **trip**: the weather, what is open, lodging and camping windows, and the crowd calendar the firefall creates. It links to the firefall guide and `/firefall` for the event and repeats only the 2026 logistics in one paragraph.
- `yosemite-in-winter` covers the whole season; this piece is February-specific, in the same frame as the January and December guides.
