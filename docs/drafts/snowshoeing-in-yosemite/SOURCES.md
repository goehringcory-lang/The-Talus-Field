# Sources: snowshoeing-in-yosemite (DRAFT, unpublished)

Every page below was fetched and read on **October 16, 2026** unless marked otherwise.

## Why this topic, and why it does not cannibalize

`yosemite-in-winter` gives Dewey Point and the ranger walks one paragraph each, and `yosemite-winter-hikes` deliberately stops at the snow line ("Above the Valley rim the season belongs to snowshoes and skis... a different article"). No article names the park's three marked areas, Crane Flat's routes, rentals, the guided walks, or the winter permit and camping rules. This draft is that article. Its intent is "where and how do I snowshoe"; the winter guide's is "what is the winter season like". The one shared keyword, "dewey point snowshoe", should live on one page only (see `entry.js`).
