Russell When the ancient Indian squaw, Lucy, died a few years ago, Yosemite valley lost her only representative of that original band of aborigines that resided here when the white man discovered Yosemite in 1851. Information regarding another survivor from the original Yosemite tribe has reached the Yosemite Museum occasionally during the years that have elapsed since Lucy's death.
