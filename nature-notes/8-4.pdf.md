GALEN'S BUGLE
"As the twig is bent, so the tree
inclines."—And thereby hangs the
tale of Galen's Bugle in the Mariposa grove.
No doubt everyone has noticed
that, whereas saplings grow straight
up from the soil, mature forest
trees seem to lean away from the
perpendicular, at least to some degree. Nature's plan would seem to
indicate that trees growing under
favorable conditions should 'pull directly away from the force of
gravity; yet, throughout the coniferous forests of the Sierra few
have held perpendicularity to maturity. The outstanding,. offsetting
factor here would appear to be the
struggle for light which causes the
topshoot, or leader, to reach towards the open spaces. Trees leaning in all directions are so commonplace as not to be considered
unusual growths, or even freakish.
In fact., conifers of truly unusual
form are such rarities as to attract
much attention.
