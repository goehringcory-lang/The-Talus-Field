NATURE STUDY PARTY CLIMBS MT. DANA
Leaving Happy Isles at
six thirty on the morning. of August fourth, a
party of twelve hikers led by a nature-guide walked twenty-five miles and
climbed six thousand feet on their way to Boothe Lake Hikers' Camp, where they
spent the first night.
The following morning. they explored the chain of beautiful lakes that
lie along a bench some two hundred feet above the hikers' camp, In the afterno
they followed down the Ireland Creek Trail to

if one start with a fair knowledge of the plants in

many trains of reminiscences along the trailside - the writer has been tempted to

Our story must date back to the end of the glacial period, some 20,000
years ago, when both YOSEMITE and Cape Cod started wi th. a clean slate. The soil

lakes were formed in back
