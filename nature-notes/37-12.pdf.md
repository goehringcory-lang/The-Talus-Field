is equipped. In addition to visible
equipment such as a radio, red light,
etc., the ranger carries a collapsible

containing food rations (similar to,

his truck, shoulder his pack, pick up
an axe, shovel and other fire fighting equipment (also carried in the
truck) and be gone for several days
if necessary.
