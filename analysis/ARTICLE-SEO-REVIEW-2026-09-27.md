## Implemented writing decisions

- Answer the visitor's question in the opening paragraph. Keep the author's personal experience where it helps a choice, after the basic logistics.
- Name the start and finish of a hike. Separate trail distance from parking walks, total ascent from descent, and road status from trail access.
- Replace rhetorical questions, unsupported superlatives, casualty statistics, and scolding with an action a reader can take.
- Use specific place names and descriptive internal links. Keep the existing dry, declarative house voice, without em-dashes or exclamation marks in the edited copy.
- Preserve every existing h2, its order, and any authored id. No article URL changes.
- Preserve publication dates; update modification dates and body cache versions for the four substantive edits. Refresh word counts from rendered prose, excluding SVG/style/script text.

Examples: “what the internet isn't telling you” becomes “distance, difficulty and route guide”; a long dining preamble becomes quick meals and sit-down options; “every other gateway has a road problem” becomes a recommendation for the low-elevation approach with a conditions check.

## Google guidance and implementation

[Google's title guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link) supports distinct, concise titles that describe the page. [Snippet guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/snippet) supports relevant descriptions, while Google may select different text for a query. These changes improve relevance and clarity; they do not guarantee ranking.

[Google's current documentation updates](https://developers.google.com/search/updates) say FAQ rich results stopped appearing May 7, 2026, and that llms.txt does not affect Google visibility or rankings. FAQ data was corrected to agree with the body, not expanded as a rich-result tactic. The existing generated llms mirrors remain synchronized for their other consumers. No keyword stuffing or sitewide schema changes were introduced.

New contextual links include Mist Trail to the parking and shuttle guides, Four Mile/Panorama to the parking guide, and gateway towns to current conditions. Existing related-article lists remain valid. Canonicals, section anchors, and public URLs remain unchanged.

## Recommended next writing passes

1. Apply the same opening pattern to Yosemite Falls, Clouds Rest, and Cathedral Lakes: start, turnaround, distance, gain, time, and access constraint before scenery or personal history.
2. Review the dining roster separately with business websites. Its August 2026 provenance remains explicit; this pass does not certify every business's hours, menu, or continued operation. Short summaries should keep choices near the visitor's lodging or route.
3. Give gateway driving times a dedicated route audit. Existing figures remain estimates, not live navigation; avoid promises about prices or last-minute rooms in future refreshes.
4. Preserve personal recommendations, but remove claims about what “most people,” “the internet,” or all competing guides do unless evidence is supplied. A specific reason to choose a route is more useful than a superlative.
