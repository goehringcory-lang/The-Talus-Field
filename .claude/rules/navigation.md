# The masthead navigation, the compact bar, the phone Menu sheet, KEEP_GOING, /explore and site search

Moved from the root CLAUDE.md (Editorial site conventions); loads when a matching file is touched.

- **Navigation (July 2026 pass; one masthead since the September 2026 design rollout; the six-label pass, October 2026).** `NAV_GROUPS` in `components.jsx` is the site's map of primary destinations, and its six labels are the site's one navigation vocabulary: **Plan / Hikes & permits / Conditions / Seasons / Map / Library**, in that order in the masthead (`HOME_NAV`), in the phone Menu sheet, heading the footer's columns, sectioning `/explore`, and in the archive's flat row (`DESIGN_NAV`). They replaced the September four words (Plan a trip / Park now / Map / Read): Park now split into **Conditions** (the live pages: the Bulletin, `/conditions` with the road alerts, webcams, the Tioga opening, the entrance-waits feature) and **Seasons** (`/dates`, the seasonal section, fall color, firefall, moonbow, frazil ice); **Hikes & permits** is new (the Half Dome lottery first, four hand-picked hike articles as fixed links, Kit; it lands on the trails section); Read became **Library**, which also holds the bookshelf ("Books ↗", moved out of the top row), the archive, films, the Sunday Letter, Search (its `aside`) and `/explore`. Five groups are menus (keys `plan`, `hikes`, `conditions`, `seasons`, `library`); Map is a plain link. To the right of the labels: the search box, **Field Guide** (a text link to `/guide`, a jump to `#field-guide` on `/`; it replaced the "Get the app ↗" button) and **Subscribe**, the masthead's one primary button (`/newsletter`, a jump to `#home-newsletter` on `/`). A group is `{ key, label, route, cta, blurb, aside, feature, columns }`: `route` is the landing page, the panel's first link (its `cta`) and the phone sheet's "More" link, so **no column repeats it and no link appears twice in one group**; `aside` is a small block under the panel's lede (the trip selector, road alerts, search), `feature` the panel's third track (`NavWaitsFeature` on Conditions, which mounts `EntranceWaits variant="menu"` the first time Conditions opens so a page view that never opens it never fetches the feed, and `NavNewestFeature` on Library, the three newest entries; the Plan panel's map strip retired when Map kept its own label), and a link is `{ key }` for an SPA route or `{ href }` for a real navigation, optionally with `hash` (a section on its page, scrolled to once the route has drawn: `/conditions#road-alerts`, `/planning#trip-selector`, `/conditions#cond-waits`), `onHome` (the homepage section it jumps to when the reader is on `/`) or `quick: true` (one of the three or four links the phone sheet lists under the group). **Layout:** from 880px up the masthead is two rows, the brand with the search box (its icon below 1000px), Field Guide and Subscribe on the first and the six labels on the second. **A menu label is a button, not a link**: a click, a tap, Enter or Space opens and pins its panel; a mouse resting on it opens it (hover only for `pointerType: mouse`; a 400ms close delay and a bridge under the label row carry the pointer down); clicking a hover-opened label pins it rather than closing it; ArrowDown moves into the panel; Escape, a press outside the nav or taking a link closes it. The group holding the current page carries a hairline under its label. **The phone Menu sheet (mobile menu approved by Cory, October 2026; it reverses the earlier "no hamburger" rule).** Below 880px the labels and their panels are not drawn at all (the desktop panels must never appear on a phone); the masthead row is the mark, the Search icon, Subscribe and a **Menu** button, and under 480px the name goes to screen readers so the row fits. Menu opens `NavSheet`: a full-height sheet listing the six groups in order, each with its `quick` links and a "More" link to the group's landing page (Map is one link), then Field Guide and Books at the bottom. Its accessibility contract, all in `HomeMasthead`: the button carries `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls="hp-sheet"`; the sheet is `role="dialog" aria-modal="true" aria-label="Site menu"` **only while open** (its element is always in the markup, empty and `hidden` while closed, so `aria-controls` always resolves, and the role is dropped while closed because app.jsx's `/` shortcut stands down whenever a modal dialog is in the document); focus moves into the sheet when it opens, Tab and Shift+Tab are trapped inside it, Escape closes it and returns focus to the Menu button, taking a link closes it, page scroll is locked (`overflow: hidden` on `<html>` and `<body>`) while it is open, and growing the window past 880px closes it. The sheet is not baked into the home shell beyond its empty element (the desktop panels already carry every link for a crawler). The archive's flat row (`.hp-nav--flat`) has no Menu button, since those pages load no JavaScript, so it stays drawn at every width and wraps on phones. **The search box** (`MastheadSearch`, `#masthead-search`, above 1000px and outside the compact bar; elsewhere the header's Search icon link stands in) is a combobox over `window.searchCatalog`: the search bundle loads the first time the box takes focus, never at boot, exact matches come first and the nearest-word correction only when they find nothing, an empty box offers four jump links, Enter on a highlighted result opens it and Enter otherwise (or the last row) hands the query to `/search`. **The compact bar**: past the bottom of the masthead's slot the header is fixed to the top as one row (`.is-stuck`), and the slot keeps its height so nothing moves (no CLS). From 880px up the row is the mark, the six labels, the Search icon and Subscribe (the name from 1200px, the article's section as a crumb from 1440px; the search box and Field Guide sit out of it); below 880px it is the phone row at 52px. It is **pinned** above 760px (shown whenever the masthead is out of view) and **headroom** on phones and on `NAV_HEADROOM_ROUTES` (shown on a scroll up only), which is `/stay`, whose booking bar is also fixed to the top and drops beneath the compact bar while it shows. `<html data-nav-mode>` sets the in-page jump offset (`scroll-padding-top` only where pinned) and `<html data-nav-compact>` lowers the reading bar; the bar is off on `/map`. There is no phone tab row. Everything else (About and Contact under the footer's about block; Directory, Advertise, Widget, Group codes and the legal pages in its bottom bar) lives in the `Footer` and on `/explore`. **The footer** heads its columns with the same six labels in the same order, carries a "Tools" line under the grid (Map, Field Guide, Conditions widget), and is hand-kept rather than derived from `NAV_GROUPS`, because it lists more than the menus do. Constraints on `NAV_GROUPS`: nothing date-derived and nothing computed from the catalog may appear in its copy (the five menus bake into index.html's static home shell, whose generator runs with an empty `window.ARTICLES` and fails the build on a leaked year), and an entry with `href` instead of `key` is a real navigation and must never get a `go()` handler (that is how `/archive/` is linked). The bookshelf link (`aff: "bookshop"`) bakes as its real Bookshop.org URL because `gen-home-shell.mjs` evaluates `affiliate.js` before rendering; it used to bake as `href="#"`. Changing a label or a group means changing `HOME_NAV`, the `Footer` headings, `/explore`'s sections and its crawler prose (`HUB_PROSE["/explore"]` in `edge/seo.js`), the `<noscript>` nav in `index.html`, and the archive's `DESIGN_NAV` / `siteFooter()` together, then `run home-shell` and `run archive` (and bump `ARCHIVE_CONTENT_UPDATED`). **Breadcrumbs**: an article reads Home › Library › Section › Title and a section page Home › Library › Section, with the BreadcrumbList JSON-LD in `app.jsx` and `edge/seo.js` matching; the compact bar's crumb reads Library › Section. GA4: panel links fire `cta_click` with `home_navigation` / `site_navigation` (a link to `/guide` fires `guide_cta_click`), as do Subscribe and Field Guide (through `HomeLink`); the sheet's links fire the same events with `location: mobile_navigation`, its Books link `affiliate_click` with `aff_list: nav_sheet`, and opening the sheet fires `nav_menu_open`; the search box fires `nav_search_submit` and `nav_search_pick` with the masthead locations (ARCHITECTURE.md). **`/explore` is the site index** (`page-explore.jsx`): every reader-facing destination with a line on what it is, sectioned by the six labels and then the Field Guide and the journal's own pages, counts drawn live from the catalog, linked from the footer, the 404 page, and `/search`. **`KEEP_GOING`** (also `components.jsx`) is the curated onward-links table keyed by route; `app.jsx` mounts `<KeepGoing>` once under `<main>`, so a page ends in three or four deliberate next steps instead of nothing. A route absent from the table renders nothing — articles have their own related rails, and the home and legal pages want no block. Breadcrumbs are on every standing page.
- **Site search (`/search`)** is client-side over `window.ARTICLES` plus a hand-maintained list of standing pages in `page-search.jsx` — no index file to fetch, no third-party widget. It searches **titles, deks, and section names, not article bodies**: bodies are lazy-loaded per slug, so a full-text index would mean pulling all of them down or shipping a fourth generated mirror. The page says so rather than letting a body-text miss read as "the article doesn't exist". `?q=` is mirrored with `replaceState`, not `pushState`, so typing doesn't bury the previous page under one history entry per keystroke, and `edge/seo.js` marks the route `noindex, follow` (a results page is thin, duplicative content, and every `?q=` is another near-identical URL) — which is also why it is NOT in the sitemap table in `gen-seo-artifacts.mjs`, only in `STATIC_ROUTES` for link checking.
