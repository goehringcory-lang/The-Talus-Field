# The homepage (`/`), its static shell and the shared `Hp*` design components

Moved from the root CLAUDE.md (Editorial site conventions); loads when a matching file is touched.

- **The homepage: the visitor-first page (September 2026 redesign, PR #410).** The August 2026 broadsheet (edition rule, index, two-column body with a sticky rail) is gone. `/` is now a hand-written landing page for a first-time visitor, all in `page-home.jsx`, and the source of truth for the design system every route now uses (see the next bullet). Top to bottom: `HomeHero` (eyebrow, h1, intro, two anchor buttons to `#home-start-here` and `#field-guide`, the Valley View photo), a **utility strip** (`.hp-utility`: conditions, entry and reservations, getting around), **Start Here** (`#home-start-here`: one featured card plus three rows, the four first-trip articles), the **Field Guide section** (`#field-guide`, `.hp-product`: three benefit lines, two phone captures from `img/guide/screens/`, the `/guide` button with the price), the **Sunday Letter** (`#home-newsletter`: `NewsletterInline` with its new `inputLabel` prop, `location: home_newsletter`, tag `home`), and **the journal** (`.hp-journal-grid`: six hand-picked pages, chosen in October 2026 from a three-month Search Console export plus the affiliate markup each page carries; re-curate from a fresh export, the reasoning is in the comment above the grid). Nine things are load-bearing. (1) **The masthead is `HomeMasthead` in `components.jsx`, on every route** (see the Navigation bullet for its structure). `Header` is a thin wrapper that returns it, and on `/` it is what the shell generator bakes: a top strip, the brand, the four navigation labels (Plan a trip, Park now, Map, Read), the search box and a "Get the app" button. On `/` two panel links become jumps to the homepage's own sections (`onHome` in `NAV_GROUPS`: Start here to `#home-start-here`, the Sunday Letter to `#home-newsletter`) and the button jumps to `#field-guide`. (2) **The three menu groups of `NAV_GROUPS` (Plan a trip, Park now, Read) bake into the home shell** as panels (hidden until opened), so the generator's year-leak guard covers their copy and a change to any of them needs `run home-shell` (`--check` fails otherwise). The Read panel's Newest column and the Park now panel's live waits read the catalog and the NPS feed at render or on first open, so the shell bakes the first empty and the second as dashes; both are safe only because every panel is closed at first paint. (3) **`HomeLink`** (`components.jsx`) is the page's only anchor component: modified and middle clicks keep native behaviour, a `#` href scrolls to the section and focuses it (the three sections carry `tabIndex={-1}`; smooth unless reduced motion), any other href goes through `go()` (`/articles/<slug>` becomes `a:<slug>`, everything else its route key), and every click fires `cta_click` (or `guide_cta_click` for `/guide`) with `location` and `target`. The locations are `home_navigation`, `home_hero`, `home_content` and `home_newsletter`; `home_index`, `home_dispatch`, `home_rail` and `home_start_here` are retired and listed in ARCHITECTURE.md, never to be reused. (4) **Everything on the page is hand-written, including the numbers.** The Field Guide section is the shared `HpGuideBand`, whose default points (`HP_GUIDE_POINTS` in `components.jsx`) and button print 44 stops, 57 day hikes, 72 Secret Guide entries, $3.99, 18 months and the 30-day guarantee as literal copy on every page that mounts the band, and the four Start Here reads and six journal picks are literal slugs that mirror `window.START_HERE` in `data.js` by hand. Nothing on the page reads the catalog or the API (the old index counts are gone), so a price change or a change to the guide's content means editing `HP_GUIDE_POINTS` and `HpGuideBand`, a re-curated Start Here means editing this file. Since September 2026 `apps/guide/scripts/check-sales-counts.ts` (in `run check`) holds every printed stop, hike, Secret Guide and entry count on the site, the band included, to the guide's own content; nothing checks the price copy or the Start Here slugs. (5) **Nothing is asked for twice** still holds: one guide section, one letter unit, and no lodging unit at all (the rail's Expedia link and its `page_home` `aff_list` are retired). (6) **The page is built from the shared design components** (`HpHeading`, `HpRow`, `HpCard`, `HpGuideBand`, `HpLetter`, beside `HomeLink`), and every change to them is diffed against the homepage at 1440, 1024 and 390: it is the reference render for the whole site. (7) The homepage **fetches nothing**: `HomeBulletin` and `HOME_BULLETIN_URL` are gone, so `bulletin.json` has two readers (`page-now.jsx` and `page-tioga-opening.jsx`) and `check-asset-freshness.mjs` checks those two. (8) The **resume band** (`ResumeReading`, the `resume_shown` and `resume_click` events, the only writer of `tfg.read.resume`) and `DeferredSection` are gone. `page-article.jsx` still writes `tfg.read.last` and still honours `tfg.read.resume` if it is ever set, but no surface sets it now. (9) The old `.home-*` CSS (edition rule, index, body, rail) was deleted in the design rollout's cleanup. Two standing rules: copy in the hero and the masthead stays free of years and month names (the shell guard), and every `HomeLink` href is either a route the router knows or the id of a section on this page.
