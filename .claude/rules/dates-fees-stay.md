# /dates, /international, live parking, road alerts and /stay

Moved from the root CLAUDE.md (Editorial site conventions); loads when a matching file is touched.

- **The five-feature pass (September 2026, `FEATURE-RESEARCH-2026-09.md`).** Two new standing routes, built on the `/distances` recipe. **`/dates`** (`page-dates.jsx`) is the deadline table: `scripts/data/deadlines.json` is the single source (fourteen rows, each with its nps.gov source, a `kind` and a `confidence`), `scripts/gen-dates-ics.mjs` (`npm --prefix scripts run dates`) generates `dates-data.js` (the raw data script `PAGE_MODULES` loads on the route) and the calendar files under `/ics/` (one per fixed window plus `yosemite-dates.ics`), and the Field Guide keeps a hand mirror in `apps/guide/src/content/deadlines.ts`; `scripts/check-deadlines.mjs` (in `run check`) fails if the mirror, the generated files or the JSON disagree. Three rules: the generator never reads the clock (`--check` byte-compares, so the Half Dome cables rule is emitted for the explicit `RULE_YEARS`, extended by hand each autumn); trip-relative rows (`relative`, `release-15th`) are resolved in the browser from the reader's dates by the functions in `page-dates.jsx`, never pre-generated; the interest chips under the capture switch the Buttondown tag between `date-halfdome` / `date-wilderness` / `date-camping` / `date-roads`, so the Sunday letter can carry a dated nudge to exactly who asked. **`/international`** (`page-international.jsx`) carries the 2026 non-resident fee: `fees-data.js` holds the fee table (`window.FEES`, with sources and `verified`) and the deterministic calculator (`window.calcEntryFees`), and `scripts/check-fee-calculator.mjs` (in `run check`) sweeps all 11,232 input combinations and recomputes the gate arithmetic independently. The interpretive call, one $250 pass covers a car, is stated in the file header with its two sources; change the table and the header together or the check fails. Neither page prints a year in its nav copy (the shell generator forbids it). **Live parking** is the shared `ParkingNow` in `components.jsx`, reading the API Worker's `/api/parking` under the waits posture (nothing rendered when silent or past 60 minutes, source-and-age stamp on every render); it is mounted on `/conditions` and inside the Roads & areas card on `/now`. **The arrival line**: every `TRIP_MONTHS` row carries `arrive`, quoted from the crowd-forecast article's own gate guidance, `buildTripPlan` returns it as `plan.arrival`, intent.jsx renders it under the day plan, and `check-intent-tags.mjs` asserts every month has one and every plan with a known month carries it. **Road alerts**: the captures on `/tioga-opening` (`alert-tioga`) and `/conditions` (`alert-roads`) now promise a triggered email, not the weekly letter; the Worker's road watch (see `workers/CLAUDE.md`) emails the operator on a confirmed change, the daily road alert drafter (`.claude/skills/road-alert/SKILL.md`, reusing the Worker's watch logic through `workers/scripts/road-watch.mts`) puts a Buttondown draft addressed only to the tag in the dashboard (`scripts/buttondown-alert.mjs`), and the owner sends it. Nothing is sent automatically.
- **`/stay` is the lodging board, and the one page built to sell (September 2026 redesign, then the booking-first pass).** `page-stay.jsx` opens with the design system's split head: the copy, a search panel (place plus optional dates) and one-tap town searches on the left, and the Half Dome photograph on the right carrying this season's answer (the park's month, America/Los_Angeles, never a year). Then a four-promise trust strip, the page's jump links, **six picks** ("if you read nothing else"), **find your base** (the road-first picker ringing towns on a schematic of the four corridors, its answer plate, and a six-town comparison table with each drive on one 0 to 120 minute scale and a search per row), the in-park cards, the hold-watch-swap sold-out band, the four corridors with their towns and badged named stays, the seasons with photographs and the current one marked, the booking facts, and a closing search. A **sticky search bar** appears only between the head search leaving the screen and the closing search arriving (top on wide screens, bottom on phones, where it lifts `.totop`). Four rules on top of the four at the head of the file (no year or rate, in-park books through the concessioner, a photo only of the place, links search the town and never the property): (1) **one colour means one action** — `.stay-book`, the filled button, is only ever an Expedia availability search, and the concessioner links are the outlined `.stay-ghost`; the page keeps its own `--stay-book` token so the booking colour is defined in one place; (2) **one search, three places** — the head panel, the sticky bar and the closing ask share one state in `StayPage`, and they and `/planning`'s lodging search (`planning_search`) are the only links on the site that carry dates into an Expedia URL, only as a valid pair; (3) **every pick and badge is a quotation** — the picker, `STAY_TOP_PICKS`, and the `badge` fields on `IN_PARK` and `PROPERTIES` restate `GATEWAYS`, the corridor intros, `SEASONS` or the gateway article, so a new one needs a published sentence behind it, and the table draws only published drive ranges (Fish Camp has no Valley time, so no bar); (4) an in-park card's concessioner button stays primary, and its optional `fallback` line ("Gone for your dates?") searches the nearest gateway town and names the drive, which `/affiliate` states; the Wawona Hotel carries neither. `ExpediaBanner` is still **one** placement, between the corridors. Every booking link carries a `stay_*` `aff_list`, inventoried in ARCHITECTURE.md. The Mariposa and Groveland photographs (the 1854 courthouse; Main Street, which is Highway 120) came through `fetch-guide-photos.mjs --target=editorial` and are credited on the page.
