# The Nature Notes archive, the film archive and the event-page system

Moved from the root CLAUDE.md (Editorial site conventions); loads when a matching file is touched.

- **The Nature Notes archive (`/archive`)** is the 1922-onward run of the National Park Service's *Yosemite Nature Notes* bulletin: 512 issues, ~1.87 million words, transcribed from the scans at yosemite.ca.us. Sources are the markdown in `nature-notes/` (excluded from the asset upload by the `*.md` rule in `.assetsignore`); `scripts/gen-archive.mjs` turns them into committed static HTML at `/archive/<year>/vol-<v>-no-<n>/`, plus year indexes, a landing page, its own `archive.css`, and its own `sitemap.xml` (referenced from `robots.txt` — 569 URLs would swamp the editorial sitemap). Parsing and text repair live in `scripts/lib/nature-notes.mjs`; read its header comment before touching either file. **These are NOT SPA routes** — they are real files served off the asset layer, so the Worker never runs for them, every page carries its own complete `<head>`, and any editorial link into the archive must be a plain `<a href="/archive/">` with no `go()` handler. `/styles.css` is linked without a `?v=` on purpose (it is served with a 5-minute TTL, and baking the shared version in would rewrite all 512 pages on every unrelated CSS tweak); `check-cache-busters.sh` only inspects `index.html`, so nothing is being smuggled past a guard. **Each issue page and the landing page end in one ask** (`askBlock` in the generator, `.arc-ask`): the Sunday letter, then one line about the Field Guide. It was the largest indexed surface on the site with no capture and no product CTA at all (CODE-AUDIT-2026-08 §5.3). Four rules, documented at the block: one ask, after the read, never inside the transcription; no JavaScript (these pages load none, so the form is a plain POST that opens the confirmation in a new tab, and the Buttondown `tag` — `archive` / `archive-index` — is the only attribution this surface will ever have, since there is no GA4 here); the guide line's count is read from the guide's own content with the same regex `check-archive-citations.mjs` uses, so it cannot drift, and drops the number rather than printing a wrong one if that shape changes; and nothing dated goes in the block, because it is baked into 512 files that regenerate only when the archive does. `--check` is wired into `npm run check`, as is `check-archive-citations.mjs`, which verifies that every `history` citation in the Field Guide's stops resolves to a real issue page here (see "Archive notes on stops" in `apps/guide/CLAUDE.md`). Two decisions worth keeping: dates are read from each issue's own masthead on **page one only** (volume 40 number 1 reprints the July 1922 issue on page two, and a whole-document search dates it to 1922), and the earliest date on the cover wins over the most precise one (volume 17 number 3 reads "March, 1938" above a photo caption dated October 4, 1933). The 54 issues whose covers carry no legible date get their volume's median year and render a visible "(year inferred)" marker — never present an inferred date as a fact. Build stats and the full inferred-date list are in `scripts/data/nature-notes-report.md`.
- **Film archive** (`page-films.jsx` + `videos-data.js`, `/films` route): the NPS *Yosemite Nature Notes* series as `window.NATURE_NOTES`. Since the September 2026 theater redesign the page has one large screen (`FilmsTheater`) under the head, a subject selector over a strip of small thumbnails, and the archive by subject below; picking any thumbnail or card puts that film on the screen and starts it. It is still click-to-load: only YouTube thumbnails load (the screen asks for `maxresdefault` and falls back to `hqdefault`, since eight uploads have no maxres) until the reader presses play or picks a film, then a `youtube-nocookie.com` iframe replaces the still. `youtubeId` values were verified against the published uploads; do not edit one without checking the video it points to. Deks are original Talus Field copy, not NPS copy.
- **The event-page system and Nature Notes films (September 2026 visual pass).** `/tioga-opening`, `/half-dome-lottery` and `/itineraries` were rebuilt on `/firefall`'s layout: the page root carries `hp-event` beside its own class, which gives it Firefall's tokens and every `.ff-*` rule (full-width photo cover behind `HpPageHead`, fact row, jump list, alternating `.ff-band` sections, splits, timeline, rule cards, hour list, FAQ); each page adds only its own pieces in its layer (`.tg-*`, `.hd-*`, `.itin-*`). A change to a shared `.ff-*` rule now changes four pages, so diff all four. `EventIcon` (components.jsx) is the shared line-icon set. Two rules from Firefall carry over: the filled `.ff-book` is only ever an Expedia town or park search, and every picture restates figures the page or its article already publishes (the Tioga profile, the Half Dome season and odds charts, the itinerary route maps drawn from `points.geojson` coordinates, and the month grid read from `window.TRIP_MONTHS`, which is why `/itineraries` now loads `intent-data.js`). `/tioga-opening`'s FAQ is printed on the page as `TIOGA_FAQ` and mirrored in `edge/seo.js`; change both. **Films:** `FilmEmbed` (the click-to-load YouTube facade) moved from page-films.jsx into components.jsx, and `NatureNotesFilm` wraps it as a captioned figure for pages and article bodies (17 bodies carry one or an inline explainer SVG since this pass). Copy `youtubeId` only from `videos-data.js`, never from memory; `gen-prerender.mjs` stubs the component as a plain watch-page link with no thumbnail, so nothing is fetched from YouTube before a reader presses play.
