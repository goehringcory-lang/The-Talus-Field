When

About two weeks each February, when the setting sun lines up with the fall.

Strongest colorAbout February 17 to 24The middle week: best light, biggest crowd.

The odds

Three things have to line up.

Water in the fallChanges daily
A clear westChanges hourly
Sun angleFixed: the dates
Most evenings, it does not happen.
The glow
10 minutes

It comes in the last minutes before sunset, a little before 6 p.m., and is gone within about ten minutes.

The short version

Plan for the odds.

  1. 01Book a refundable room months ahead, not weeks.
  2. 02Plan two or three evenings, not one.
  3. 03Weekdays over weekends, always.
  4. 04Dress for standing still in the cold.
